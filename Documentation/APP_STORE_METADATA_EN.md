## Promotional text
Plan your renovation with clear room measurements, practical material estimates and an organized shopping list—all on your iPhone.

## Description
Great renovation plans begin with good measurements. HomeMeasure brings room dimensions, material quantities, shopping and budgeting into one focused workspace.

**Plan by room**
Record the length, width and height of each room. Add doors and windows to estimate usable wall space and the baseboard length to purchase.

**Estimate materials**
Build estimates for tile, flooring, paint, wallpaper, baseboards and custom items. Set usable product coverage, coating layers and waste allowances to calculate whole-package quantities.

**Know what to buy**
Enter package prices, taxes and delivery costs, then review a clear project budget. Keep track of purchases across every project from one shopping list.

**Take your plan anywhere**
Preview an English-language purchase report and save it as PDF. Export and import your projects as local JSON backups using the system Files interface.

**Built for your privacy**
No account. No advertising. No cloud workspace. Projects remain on your iPhone unless you choose to export or share them.

HomeMeasure is a renovation planning tool, not a professional surveying service or live supplier quotation system. Verify actual dimensions, product coverage and local pricing before purchasing.

## Suggested review notes
HomeMeasure is an English-language, portrait-only offline room and material estimator. No login or server access is required. Reviewers may create a project, add a room and door/window measurements, add tile/paint materials, adjust waste and packaging, mark items purchased, export a PDF purchase report and use Settings to export or import a JSON backup. The photo picker is optional. Room photos are not included in JSON backups.
