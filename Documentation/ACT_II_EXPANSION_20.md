# Act II — The Twenty Expansion Characters

This document records the second acts added to the twenty characters introduced in the cast expansion.
Each journey now has eight chapters: four in Act I and four in Act II. Every Act II chapter offers two authored
choices and carries two branch-aware callbacks drawn from the player's own earlier decisions. Chapter openings
stay compatible with either preceding branch; the consequence appears in the outcome and the callback.
