## Art and integration

Original portrait and environment renders are preserved in Artwork/Expansion8/Source. prepare_expansion8_assets.py fits portraits to 768×1024, derives matching 512×512 avatars and fits scene images to 1200×640. Alpha is retained for portraits and avatars. Each scene is independently generated; existing art is not reused for a new identity. Preview.jpg renders the exact installed asset files. Backend canon and full authored biographies were regenerated from the JSON.
