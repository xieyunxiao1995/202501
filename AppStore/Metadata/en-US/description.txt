Find a world you want to return to.

Tideverse brings together 40 original fictional adult characters and 328 authored chapters. Explore mysteries, ocean adventures, creative communities and unexpected discoveries. Each chapter offers two choices, and later scenes can remember the path you took.

YOUR CHOICES, YOUR PATH
Begin a journey without creating an account. Read offline, make a decision, and return to your saved progress. Your chapter choices remain separate from optional AI conversation.

KEEP A STORY MOMENT
Revisit the outcome of each decision in Journeys. Preview and export a Story Moment as a PDF, or back up your local records as a JSON archive.

CONVERSATIONS WITH CONTEXT
Explore authored scene beats in Chats. Optional live AI replies require an available service, an internet connection and your permission before selected conversation context is sent for processing.

MEMORIES YOU CONTROL
Review, edit, pin or delete character memory notes. Choose an optional photo yourself, and extract visible text on-device before deciding what to send.

MADE FOR A QUIET READING BREAK
A midnight-blue interface, illustrated characters, searchable journeys and chapter progress keep the focus on your next decision.

All characters are fictional AI personas. They are not real people and do not have independent lives outside the app.
