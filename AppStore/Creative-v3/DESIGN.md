# Tideverse reference-led campaign v3

Surface mode: Persuade. Static iPhone App Store artwork only. App UI and installation icon unchanged.

Reference selection: Tavo iPhone_0 for a large character breaking the device silhouette, Tavo iPhone_1 and iPhone_3 for layered device compositions, Swerve iPhone_1 and iPhone_2 for bold concise headings and enlarged UI excerpts. Char.AI inspected but its technology-network backdrop and unrelated character-creation claims do not fit Tideverse. References are visual inspiration, never sources of shipped artwork or product claims. No iPad reference was used.

Visual system: black-blue #040A16, white #FAFCFF, sky blue #79C8FF, secondary blue #B9CDE2. Manrope Bold headings at 112px with tight tracking, centered in two lines; 38px Manrope supporting sentence. Real illustrated environments supply subtle dark backdrops. Large metallic iPhone frames dominate; actual screenshots are scaled proportionally. Hardware silhouette and camera island are drawn device chrome, not app controls.

Composition sequence: 01 angled Discover device and Mira in foreground; 02 three layered device screens with Jonah and Elara at canvas edges; 03 chat device with exact enlarged authored invitation crop and Vera foreground; 04 Journeys device with exact enlarged journey-card crop. No fabricated live replies, ratings, generated UI, character-creation tools or service-speed claims.

Character crop boundaries extend past the canvas, avoiding flat torso seams. Display headings sit in a consistent legible top band, without decorative badges or section numbering. Output is 1320 x 2868 sRGB opaque PNG.

Code-led layout with original bundled art. Scope is a replacement of the store campaign only. Previous v1 and v2 preserved.
