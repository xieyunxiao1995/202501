# Tideverse: illustrated story campaign

Mode: Persuade. Scope: this standalone App Store campaign only, not the app's UI design system.

Direction: original characters inhabit the story environments around a true product screen. Editorial typography pairs Cormorant Garamond Semibold display with Manrope body and brand. Character art stays recognizable and uses its actual alpha; no synthesized product UI. The four posters form a sequence: explore, meet, converse, return.

Palette: midnight #071427; blue-black #101D32; teal-black #071B29; warm ivory #F5EDD8; pale azure #9DD9FA; muted blue #C4D3DF.

Composition: 1320 × 2868 portrait; 80px main text inset; 166–185px display; 34–46px support. Character portraits occupy large middle-ground regions; original environments fill the scene; screenshots are proportionally scaled and slightly tilted with soft offset shadows. Brand footer/number badges and decorative orbit lines from v1 are removed. Small brand wordmark appears at the top. Raster text is intentional for this static store art.

Truth: 40 fictional AI characters; screenshots are actual existing UIRefinement captures. Conversation poster shows the authored opening, clearly labeled in the original screen. No fabricated live responses, ratings, testimonials, or service guarantees.

Process: user selected character/story focus. Impeccable launcher download did not finish; direct project-context fallback used. Code-led AppKit composition of existing original illustrations and screenshots. No generative image edits. Fonts fetched from Google Fonts, OFL included. The actual app design remains unchanged.
