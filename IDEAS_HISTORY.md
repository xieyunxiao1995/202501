## Entry 001 - 2026-03-14

### App Name
**CP嗒啧** (13 characters)

### Concept
Kintsugi Artisan Companion - An app for practitioners of the Japanese art of repairing broken pottery with gold lacquer. Users photograph their repair projects, receive AI-guided technique suggestions, and build a gallery of their golden seams.

### Target Audience
- Ceramic artists and pottery hobbyists
- Art therapy practitioners
- Wabi-sabi philosophy enthusiasts
- Museum conservators
- Meditation practitioners

### Core Formula
[Hyper-niche hobby: Kintsugi] + [Photo input of broken pottery] + [AI technique/narrative generation] + [Local project archive]

### Killer Feature
"Crack Narrative Generator" - AI analyzes photographed broken pieces and generates poetic narratives for each crack line, suggesting philosophical meanings and aesthetic repair approaches.

### Primary Color
#C17F59 (Warm Terracotta)

### UI Design Language
"Organic Asymmetry" - Inspired by handmade ceramics and natural imperfection. Asymmetrical layouts, ceramic-surface shadows, gold accents, handcrafted shapes, ink-wash textures.

### AI Persona
**Kintsu** - Master Kintsugi Guide. Wise, patient, poetically inclined, encouraging, technique-focused. Expert in Kintsugi techniques and wabi-sabi philosophy.

### AI Service
DeepSeek (api.deepseek.com)

### Page Count
21 screens total

### Why This Concept Passes Apple 4.3
1. Extremely hyper-niche (Kintsugi practitioners are 0.001% of population)
2. Rich visual interaction (photo capture, gallery, detail views)
3. Complex AI integration (technique advice, narrative generation)
4. Unique custom UI (Organic Asymmetry design system, no Material Design)
5. Deep functionality (21 screens, multiple workflows)
6. No existing direct competitors on App Store
7. Clear utility beyond simple content consumption
